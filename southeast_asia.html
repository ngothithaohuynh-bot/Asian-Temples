Spiritual Landmarks of Asia

Historic Pagodas and Temples Across Asia

Asian temple architecture blends spiritual symbolisms, lotus spires, golden leaves, and intricate woodwork. Here are several highlights of the spiritual monuments featured in our gallery:

Tran Quoc Pagoda (Vietnam)

Located on an islet in West Lake, Hanoi, Tran Quoc Pagoda is the oldest temple in the city, dating back over 1,500 years to the 6th century.

Kiyomizu-dera (Japan)

Kiyomizu-dera is an iconic ancient Buddhist temple in Kyoto, famous for its grand wooden stage built without nails, offering breathtaking views over cherry blossoms and maple trees.

Wat Arun (Thailand)

Known as the Temple of Dawn, Wat Arun stands on the banks of the Chao Phraya River with spires decorated in colorful porcelain shards.

Angkor Wat (Cambodia)

A masterpiece of Khmer architecture, Angkor Wat features central lotus towers and extensive bas-relief carvings.
